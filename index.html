VG-VRT ↗
VR · Senior Capstone

Senior capstone (3-person team, JMU ISAT 2022). A VR-based alternative to traditional vestibular rehab therapy — basically a game-ified version of the dizziness rehab exercises people with inner-ear balance disorders have to do. Standard VRT is monotonous, and because it's designed to provoke symptoms, patients quit early. So we built three games on Oculus Quest 2 that mirror the prescribed movements (eye tracking, bending to pick up objects, following objects between hands) inside an immersive environment.

The games log patient performance and feed it into a difficulty-prediction layer that scales the next session up or down automatically — therapy that actually adapts instead of staying flat. Built pre-Meta, presented at the ISAT Senior Symposium.
